The AI family tree · four ways to draw it

Option A Nested blocks · the same containment, as boxes with their names on the rim

The idea: four rounded boxes, each inside the last, each named along its own top edge so nothing can ever sit on top of anything else. Years on the right, Claude pulsing in the middle. Plain HTML, so it reflows on a phone instead of shrinking.

Option B Timeline ladder · four rows, newest at the bottom, each stepped inside the one above

The idea: a ladder you read top to bottom. Each row carries its decade, its one-line definition and three workplace examples, and is indented under the row above to say "this is a kind of that". The most informative of the four, and the easiest to read on a phone.

Option C The actual family tree · branches, with the path to Claude lit

The idea: the diagram the heading already promises. AI splits into rule-based systems and machine learning; machine learning into classic models and deep learning; deep learning into perception and generative AI; and at the bottom, the language models. A moving violet line traces the path to Claude. It also teaches something the rings cannot: the siblings, like the fraud model that is classic machine learning.

Option D Offset circles · your rings, fixed

The idea: keep the nested circles but let them touch at the bottom instead of sharing a centre. Every ring then has a wide crescent at the top where its name and one short line fit without touching the next ring. The closest to what you have; the labels still shrink with the drawing on a phone.
