The real thing, inside the course

A live GPT-2 you can look inside

This is the Transformer Explainer built by the Polo Club at Georgia Tech, shown here exactly as they publish it. It runs an actual GPT-2 (small) model in your browser: type a sentence, press Generate, and every number you see, from the embeddings to the attention maps to the output probabilities, is computed live by the model. The card in Week 1 is drawn to the same shape; this one is the genuine article.

It downloads the model the first time, a few hundred megabytes, so use a laptop on a good connection. It needs the internet, so it is the one part of the course that does not work offline. If the frame stays blank, open it directly: poloclub.github.io/transformer-explainer.
